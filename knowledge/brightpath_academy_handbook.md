# BrightPath Academy Student Handbook (Fictional Test Document)

## About BrightPath Academy
BrightPath Academy is a fictional online learning institute that offers short professional courses in data analytics, digital marketing and product management. Courses run in three intakes per year: January, May and September. Each course lasts 12 weeks and includes weekly live sessions, recorded lectures and a final capstone project.

## Admissions
Applicants must be at least 18 years old and have completed secondary school. There is no entrance exam. Applications for each intake close two weeks before the start date. After applying, candidates attend a 15-minute online counselling call to confirm the course is a good fit.

## Fees and Scholarships
The standard course fee is 24,000 rupees. Students can pay in full or in three monthly instalments of 8,500 rupees. A merit scholarship covering 25 percent of the fee is available to the top 10 percent of applicants based on a short written statement. Early-bird applicants who apply at least six weeks before the intake receive a 10 percent discount. Scholarships and early-bird discounts cannot be combined.

## Attendance Policy
Students must attend at least 75 percent of live sessions to be eligible for a certificate. If a student misses a live session, they must watch the recording within seven days and submit a short reflection note. Students who fall below 75 percent attendance can request a one-time extension from the programme coordinator.

## Refund Policy
Students can request a full refund within the first seven days of the course. Between day 8 and day 21, a 50 percent refund is available. No refunds are given after day 21. Refund requests must be sent by email to the student support team and are processed within 10 working days.

## Capstone Project
Every course ends with a capstone project completed in teams of three or four students. Teams present their project to a panel of industry mentors in week 12. The capstone counts for 40 percent of the final grade. Students who score above 85 percent overall receive a "Certificate with Distinction".

## Contact
For questions about admissions, fees or refunds, students should contact the student support team. For questions about course content, students should contact their course mentor during weekly office hours.
